The laws. What each power reads on exact values

The check. Over 12 sets of divergences from a cloud and 2 tight camps, the raw numbers give
the package's Cohesion at power 0.5, and its entropy at power 1, to within 1e-12.

Law 1. M values, exactly separate, with equal shares.
Power 1 reads 1 - H as the chance two values match, 1 / m, or (k / m - 4) / (k - 4) at this k.
Power 0.5 reads H as one equal step for each extra value, 8(m - 1) / (k - 8).

Law 2. Exactly separate values with unequal shares p.
Power 1 swaps 1 / m for the sum of p squared, the chance two values match. A stray barely counts.
Power 0.5 swaps m for the sum of root p, squared. A stray of 1% counts a fifth of a value.
So the formulas are (k sum p^2 - 4) / (k - 4) and 8((sum root p)^2 - 1) / (k - 8).

Law 3. N random values, uniform on [0, 1]. The mean over 100 sets.
Power 1 reads 1 - H as 1 / N whatever k is, so it needs no rule for k.
Power 0.5 reads H as 1 - k / (16N), so k has to grow with N. This gives the 8n rule.
