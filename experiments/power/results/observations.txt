Level 1. Each trial's entropy at power 1 and at power 0.5

Power 1 is the package's estimator, at k = 10 on its grid. Power 0.5 is the same with the root of each
density taken first. Its kernel then reaches twice as far, so the package's grid cuts its tail off.
'k = 20' and 'wide grid', which runs 1.5 past each end, are the two repairs for that. Power 1 at
k = 20 is the control for the first.

The check. Over 50 random ensembles, power 1 here gives the package's incoherence, and power 0.5
gives the package's density_entropy at power 0.5, to within 1e-5. Its limits come from 200,001
evenly spaced observations, where the package integrates exactly.

Agreement with Shannon entropy. The papers justify the estimator against Shannon entropy, binned,
as a share of its most. Rank is the Spearman rank agreement, so 1 is the same order. Gap is the
mean gap, so 0 is the same value.
